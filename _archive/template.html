Plain-English glossary


SyncA moment when a sales channel is updated from the master system.


UrgencyUrgent, Soon or Low: how far from normal a sync is.


Stale syncA channel that hasn’t updated for far too long.


Oversell riskA channel is selling faster than the stock it shows.


Precision / RecallOf the alerts raised, how many were real; of the real problems, how many were found.


AutoencoderA small neural network trained to rebuild normal syncs. Syncs it can’t rebuild are flagged.
